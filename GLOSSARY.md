# NVR operator experience

Language for viewing cameras and investigating recorded footage.

## Language

**Live workspace**:
The operator's current arrangement of live camera feeds.
_Avoid_: Live configuration page

**Playback workspace**:
The operator's current arrangement of recorded camera feeds viewed against a shared time position.
_Avoid_: Footage Search page

**Selected camera**:
A camera included in the current workspace's view.
_Avoid_: Active camera, when referring only to inclusion in the view

**Active tile**:
The video position currently targeted by tile controls.
_Avoid_: Selected camera, when referring only to control focus

**Auto layout**:
An arrangement whose grid dimensions adjust to the number of selected cameras and available viewing space.
_Avoid_: Required grid-size selection

**Saved view**:
A reusable arrangement of cameras that retains their order and the choice of Auto or manual layout.
_Avoid_: Grid size, when referring to the whole saved arrangement

**Site branding**:
The installation-wide application name, logos and brand colors presented to its users.
_Avoid_: Camera brand, when referring to the application's identity
