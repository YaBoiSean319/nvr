## What to build

Deliver reproducible performance evidence and an honest acceptance record for the completed overhaul, protecting recording while qualifying the exact tested workload.

Design reference and implementation handoff: https://github.com/YaBoiSean319/nvr/tree/docs/workspace-overhaul-2026-10-22/docs/workspace-overhaul

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Instrument and measure p95 input-to-painted-feedback ≤100 ms for clicks/filtering/panels/layout with at least 200 configured-camera entries and background exports.
- [ ] On declared reference hardware/network with healthy supported streams, measure p95 first decoded fresh live frame ≤2 seconds and committed seek to actual decoded requested footage ≤1 second; a spinner is not a media success.
- [ ] Exercise 1/4/9/16 displayed-camera workloads and representative CPU-only exports; report cold/warm runs, sample counts, p50/p95, errors/timeouts, stream profiles/codecs/bitrates and workstation/NVR/network/storage context.
- [ ] Optimize measured bottlenecks without sacrificing recording priority, resource/admission limits or evidence correctness; inventory browsing must not create 200 viewer sessions.
- [ ] Verify connection/decoder/media-lease cleanup and rapid-seek cancellation under sustained churn; record CPU/RAM/disk/free-space pressure, queue delay, live drops and recording-write health.
- [ ] Keep implementation/CI verification separate from deployment qualification. Every environment-only check has an unchecked Post-merge testing required entry with steps, environment, version, owner and result/evidence fields.
- [ ] Retain target identity/camera/codec, persistent-volume upgrade/recovery, real audio/exports, low-space and original 30-day parallel reliability checks. Do not claim 200-camera recording certification from a synthetic inventory or close qualification on CI alone.
- [ ] Publish a sanitized acceptance report and operating limits. Do not include camera names/addresses/URLs, credentials, tenant identifiers or footage in reports.
