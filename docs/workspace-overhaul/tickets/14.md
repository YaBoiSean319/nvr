# [Workspace 14] Make operational health and incident actions available everywhere

Canonical issue: https://github.com/YaBoiSean319/nvr/issues/63

## Parent

https://github.com/YaBoiSean319/nvr/issues/49

## What to build

Expose trustworthy operational health and incident response from a compact persistent status area in every workspace.

Design reference and implementation handoff: https://github.com/YaBoiSean319/nvr/tree/docs/workspace-overhaul-2026-10-22/docs/workspace-overhaul

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Show compact camera, recording-write, storage and job signals with contextual details rather than large permanent dashboards.
- [ ] Distinguish fresh live video from successful recording writes, expected motion idle, failure and uncertainty; keep unresolved warnings visible with text/icons.
- [ ] Use existing incident grouping/acknowledgement/recovery behavior and authorized operator actions; preserve webhook and external-heartbeat semantics.
- [ ] Provide role-safe operational summary data to operators without exposing administrator-only metrics, sessions, diagnostic capture or private configuration.
- [ ] Opening warning details leads to the relevant camera/incident/settings context while preserving the current Live/Playback investigation.
- [ ] Aggregate repeated warnings and bound refresh/update work; status failures cannot freeze viewing or imply a healthy recorder.
- [ ] Test live-healthy/write-stalled, expected idle, storage pressure, incident acknowledgement/recovery and role boundaries through actual status/incident APIs and browser flows.
