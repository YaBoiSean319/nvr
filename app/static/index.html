            External host heartbeat

            The NVR sends a signed heartbeat to a receiver that must run outside this NVR host. Configure that external system to alert when heartbeats stop. “Last successful send” only confirms the NVR received a successful response; it does not prove the external checker is alerting.

              Cameras

              Live viewing

              Choose a 1, 4, 9, or 16-feed layout. Double-click a camera to add it; drag a camera into a tile to add or reorder it. Grid tiles use a configured low-resolution profile where available. Expand a feed for the main stream, then press Escape or Restore grid. Streams use your signed-in session and close when you stop viewing or leave this page. Audio starts muted, appears only when enabled by an administrator, and only one camera can be audible at a time.

              Unsaved changes. Save this view to keep the new camera order and grid size.

              Synchronized playback

              Choose up to eight cameras or groups and a time range up to 24 hours. New searches start with the past hour. Results load only when you search; playback stays paused at the earliest indexed recording, and cameras without footage remain visible. Streams stay aligned to one site-time ruler; timeline gaps distinguish expected idle, recording failure, uncertain coverage, and unindexed footage.

              Exports run as bounded background jobs. Completed downloads contain one playable video per selected camera and metadata listing actual recorded intervals and missing time. Audio is omitted.

                Search inputs changed. Existing results are stale until you search again.

                Discover cameras

                Use multicast on the camera VLAN, or enter a private IPv4 CIDR range reachable through routing. Range scans use unicast ONVIF probes and do not require the NVR host on that VLAN. Discovered devices are candidates only; nothing records until an administrator reviews and adds it.

                Range scans use bounded per-device network timeouts instead.

                Camera groups and bulk onboarding

                Browsing groups can overlap. A camera’s recording, schedule, retention, AI, and storage policies continue to inherit from its single policy group and camera overrides.

                Group recording schedule

                Intervals use ISO weekdays (0=Monday through 6=Sunday), 24-hour local time, and start-inclusive/end-exclusive boundaries. During a fall-back repeated hour both occurrences follow the interval; skipped spring-forward minutes do not occur.
