                    Discover cameras

                    Use multicast on the camera VLAN, or enter a private IPv4 CIDR range reachable through routing. Range scans use unicast ONVIF probes and do not require the NVR host on that VLAN. Discovered devices are candidates only; nothing records until an administrator reviews and adds it.

                    Range scans use bounded per-device network timeouts instead.

                    Camera groups and bulk onboarding

                    Browsing groups can overlap. A camera’s recording, schedule, retention, AI, and storage policies continue to inherit from its single policy group and camera overrides.

                    Group recording schedule

                    Intervals use ISO weekdays (0=Monday through 6=Sunday), 24-hour local time, and start-inclusive/end-exclusive boundaries. During a fall-back repeated hour both occurrences follow the interval; skipped spring-forward minutes do not occur.

                    Overnight intervals belong to their start day. Overlapping intervals are rejected. Empty intervals mean cameras are scheduled idle.
